Use Message Seller to contact the seller and agree a price. The seller then transfers the registration to you through the DVLA.

How does the transfer work?

Registrations are transferred with the DVLA, usually online. The DVLA charges an £80 fee to move a registration onto a vehicle or onto a retention certificate.
